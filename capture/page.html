      Desktop Tower Defense

      Rating:
      4.2
      Released: April 04, 2007
      Last updated: June 13, 2007
      Developer: preecep

      Tags for Desktop Tower Defense

      Description

      A live action puzzle game written for fun in flash. You have to stop your enemies, or “creeps,” from traveling all the way across the screen. Tower pieces can be purchased and placed on the map to kill the creeps before they make it across.

      How to Play

      1-6 selects a Tower & 0 deselects. U upgrades the selected Tower. S sells the selected Tower. Holding down Shift allows you to place multiple Towers one after the other. Space to toggle pause.

      FAQ

      What is Desktop Tower Defense?

      Desktop Tower Defense is a classic tower defense game developed by Paul Preece, where players defend their desktop by strategically placing towers to stop waves of enemies called creeps.

      How do you play Desktop Tower Defense?

      In Desktop Tower Defense, you build and upgrade different types of towers on a grid-like desktop surface to create paths and stop creeps from reaching the other side.

      What types of towers are available in Desktop Tower Defense?

      Desktop Tower Defense features several tower types, including squirt, pellet, dart, and glue towers, each with unique effects and upgrade options.

      Does Desktop Tower Defense have different difficulty levels?

      Yes, Desktop Tower Defense offers various difficulty modes and challenge settings, allowing players to adjust the game’s complexity and try different strategies.

      What progression or upgrade systems are in Desktop Tower Defense?

      Players in Desktop Tower Defense can earn gold by defeating creeps, which can then be spent to upgrade existing towers or build new ones for better defense.
